Purpose of the Checklist

This checklist gives simple goals that can help with confidence, wellness, organization, and self-care. The goal is not to complete everything perfectly. The goal is to keep making progress.

Weekly Glow Up Checklist

Goal Category Status
Drink more water each day Physical Wellness In Progress
Sleep at least 8 hours Health Working On It
Plan outfits ahead of time Style Started
Write down one positive thought Mental Glow Up Improving
Clean or reset your space Routine In Progress
Move your body a few times this week Fitness Started

Extra Goals to Try

  • Spend less time comparing yourself to others.
  • Keep up with a simple skincare routine.
  • Pack your bag or plan your day the night before.
  • Take care of your nails, hair, or clothes.
  • Do one thing each week that helps future you.
